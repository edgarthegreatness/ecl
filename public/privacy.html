Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Lead Desk is a private tool used by a Facebook Page owner to receive and answer enquiries sent to their own Facebook Pages. It is not a public service and has a single user: that Page owner.

What we collect

  • When you fill out a Lead Ads form on one of the connected Pages: the answers you gave on the form, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • When you message a connected Page or comment on its posts: your name as shown on Facebook, your message or comment, and the time you sent it.

How it is used

Only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on it. Your details are stored in the Page owner's private Lead Desk database (hosted on Netlify) and copied to the Page owner's private Google Sheet. Replies to form enquiries are sent by email from the Page owner's Gmail account.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than answering your enquiry.

How long it is kept

Until your enquiry is finished, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

Deleting your data

See data deletion instructions.

Contact

Send a message to the Facebook Page you contacted, and the Page owner will help.
